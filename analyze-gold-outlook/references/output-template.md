## 1. Standard report

### Conclusion first

Open with one compact paragraph that states the short-, medium-, and long-term directions, conviction, and dominant reason. Example structure:

> As of [date/time], the gold view is [short-term label], [medium-term label], and [long-term label]. The short-term price is mainly controlled by [real-yield/dollar/positioning chain], while the medium-to-long thesis depends on [Fed-liquidity/fiscal-credit/official-demand chain]. The biggest contradiction is [conflict], so [trigger] is needed before raising conviction.

### Causal analysis

Use three to five substantive paragraphs rather than a list of indicators. Connect the shock to expectations, rates, dollar/liquidity, flows, and gold. State which variable currently dominates and why. Separate market observations from inference.

### A-share miner confirmation

When relevant, explain basket purity, relative performance versus A-shares and RMB gold, breadth, volume, earnings revisions, and alternative explanations. End with a confidence judgment on whether miners are leading bullion, merely following RMB gold, or reflecting company/equity factors.

### Monitoring and invalidation

Finish with the smallest set of variables that can change the call: 10y real-yield threshold or trend, Fed path repricing, DXY trend, Treasury/funding stress, ETF/CFTC flow reversal, gold-silver state, and miner breadth or earnings revisions. State exactly what observation would invalidate each horizon view.

## 2. Fast answer

For a concise request, use two paragraphs and one compact table. The first paragraph gives the conclusion and dominant causal chain. The second explains the main contradiction and trigger. The table shows short/medium/long direction, confidence, and invalidation.

## 3. Scenario design

Scenarios must differ by causal path, not merely by arbitrary price levels. A valid bull scenario might combine falling real yields, a softer dollar, ETF inflows, and broad miner confirmation. A valid bear scenario might combine hawkish inflation repricing, rising real yields, a stronger dollar, and crowded-long liquidation. A fiscal-stress scenario may feature rising long yields and rising gold together; explain why this is not the standard real-rate regime.

Only add price targets when the user requests them and a disclosed method is available. Suitable methods include technical levels, real-yield/dollar regression with stated instability, options-implied ranges, or scenario-based valuation. Never blend methods without explaining the weights.

## 4. Reusable requests

- “Analyze the current USD gold outlook for the next month, quarter, and two years; show the causal chain and invalidation triggers.”
- “Check whether A-share gold stocks are leading gold or merely responding to RMB depreciation and equity beta.”
- “Explain why gold and 10-year Treasury yields are rising together and identify the regime.”
- “Interpret the gold-silver ratio jointly with both metal prices and positioning.”
- “Turn these indicator observations into an auditable score, then override it only if a structural reason exists.”
